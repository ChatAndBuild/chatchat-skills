# Offer And Audience

Use this reference to convert raw product facts into performance creative strategy.

## Audience Model

Define:

- Buyer: who pays.
- User: who experiences the product.
- Trigger: what makes them shop now.
- Current workaround: what they use instead.
- Desired identity: how they want to feel after buying.
- Objection: why they hesitate.
- Proof need: what would make them believe.

## Offer Model

Clarify:

- Product promise.
- Specific outcome.
- Mechanism: why it works or why it is different.
- Evidence.
- Price or value frame.
- Risk reversal.
- CTA.

## Objection Map

Common objections:

- "Will this work for me?"
- "Is it worth the price?"
- "Is this safe?"
- "Is it easy?"
- "Is this better than what I already use?"
- "Is the brand trustworthy?"
- "Can I see it in real life?"

Each strong ad should answer at least one objection visually.

## Conversion Tensions

Good TikTok ads often use one clear tension:

- I did not know this was the reason.
- I tried the cheap solution first.
- I thought this would be hard, but it was simple.
- I was skeptical until I saw the demo.
- I wanted the result without changing my whole routine.
- I did not need more options; I needed the right shortcut.
