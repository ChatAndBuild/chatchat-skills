# Human Approval Gates

Use this reference when a draft is moving toward production or paid launch.

## Draft Mode

The agent may run without interruption and label assumptions. Use this for fast concepting from a URL or rough brief.

## Production Mode

Use these gates:

1. Product facts gate.
   The advertiser confirms product name, specs, offer, availability, legal copy, and assets.

2. Claim gate.
   The advertiser confirms what can and cannot be said.

3. Angle gate.
   The advertiser chooses priority angles for production.

4. Creative draft gate.
   Before generating final media, show the selected hook, beat sheet, visual direction, voiceover/caption draft, CTA, and audio direction. Ask the advertiser to confirm or revise the draft. For production requests, explicitly say that approval moves into finished video production and tool/model selection.

5. Tool selection gate.
   After the creative draft is confirmed, ask which image, video, voiceover, BGM/SFX, and assembly tools or models to use. A brief approval confirms the creative only; it is not consent to use defaults. Verify access and capabilities yourself only after the user selects tools or explicitly delegates tool choice.

6. Asset gate.
   The advertiser approves reference images, brand assets, and any generated visual direction.

7. Launch gate.
   The advertiser approves final video, landing page match, offer validity, region, budget, and compliance risk.

## Ask For Human Input When

- The page is blocked or incomplete.
- Price, discount, or availability is unclear.
- The category is regulated or sensitive.
- The ad uses reviews, testimonials, or comparison claims.
- The creative draft is ready to move into media generation.
- Production tool/model choices or access are missing.
- The production would spend credits on generated media.
- The final asset is intended for paid launch.

## Do Not Ask When

- Producing a first draft.
- Creating clearly labeled assumptions.
- Writing prompts that are not yet executed.
- Making non-claim creative variants.

## Blocking Rule

If production tool/model choices are missing after creative approval, stop and ask. Do not begin production-stage actions such as production asset download, media generation, voice synthesis, BGM/SFX creation, render/assembly project creation, assembly, or export.
