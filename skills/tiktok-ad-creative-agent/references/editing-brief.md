# Editing Brief

Use this reference to produce a handoff for an editor, creator, or editing agent.

## Required Fields

- Platform: TikTok.
- Aspect ratio: 9:16.
- Length.
- Primary CTA.
- Product lockup.
- Subtitle style.
- Brand colors and fonts if known.
- Required legal line.
- Music or sound direction.
- Safe area notes.
- Deliverable formats.

## Editing Notes

Include:

- Cut pacing by beat.
- Where to place captions.
- Where to place product image, app screen, offer card, or end card.
- Which lines need emphasis.
- Which shots need speed ramp, zoom, crop, or freeze frame.
- Where to add social proof or review overlays.
- Where to avoid covering the product.

## Caption Rules

- Captions should be short and readable on mobile.
- Avoid full paragraphs.
- Use one idea per caption.
- Keep exact claims aligned with approved wording.
- Do not cover faces, product usage, price, or CTA.

## End Card Rules

End card should include:

- Product or brand.
- Offer if approved.
- CTA.
- Legal line if needed.
- Clean visual hierarchy.
- Enough time to read, usually 2-4 seconds.
