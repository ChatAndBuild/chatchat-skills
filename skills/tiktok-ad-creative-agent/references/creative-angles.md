# Creative Angles

Use these angles as building blocks. Pick angles based on audience tension, available proof, and the product's real strengths.

## Angle Matrix

| Angle | Best For | Opening Pattern | Proof Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem Solution | Clear pain point | "If you still deal with X..." | Demo, comparison, explanation |
| Routine Upgrade | Daily-use products | "I changed one step in my routine..." | Before routine vs after routine |
| Demo Proof | Visible transformation or function | "Watch what happens when..." | Product-in-use footage |
| Comparison | Crowded category | "I stopped buying X after trying Y..." | Side-by-side differences |
| Myth Busting | Misunderstood category | "Most people get X wrong..." | Education, mechanism |
| Founder POV | Trust and origin story | "We built this because..." | Founder credibility |
| Review-Led | Strong customer language | "This review made me try it..." | Real review excerpts |
| Ingredient / Component Story | Products with meaningful inputs | "The difference is what is inside..." | Ingredients, materials, build quality |
| Objection Crusher | High-friction purchase | "I thought it would be too X..." | Reframe, demo, guarantee |
| Offer-Led | Strong promo or bundle | "This is the best time to try..." | Price, bundle, deadline |
| Lifestyle Outcome | Identity and aspiration | "This is for people who want..." | Use case montage |
| Metaphor | Abstract benefit | "It is like giving your routine a reset..." | Visual analogy |

## Selection Rules

- Choose demo proof when the product benefit can be seen.
- Choose objection crusher when the product is expensive, unfamiliar, or hard to believe.
- Choose routine upgrade when the product fits an existing habit.
- Choose ingredient/component story when the internal quality is a differentiator.
- Choose review-led only when reviews are real and allowed.
- Choose offer-led only when the offer is strong enough to lead the ad.

## Angle Output Format

For each angle, provide:

- Angle name.
- Target audience tension.
- Hook.
- Visual proof.
- CTA.
- Risk notes.
- Why this should be tested.
