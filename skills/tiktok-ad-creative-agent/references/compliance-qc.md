# Compliance QC

Use this reference for risk review. This is not a substitute for legal or platform policy approval.

## High-Risk Categories

Be extra careful with:

- Supplements.
- Health, wellness, and beauty outcomes.
- Weight loss.
- Finance, investing, and income.
- Medical devices.
- Pets and animal health.
- Children.
- Sexual wellness.
- Housing, employment, credit, and insurance.

## Claim Review

Check every claim for:

- Source.
- Specificity.
- Evidence.
- Required qualifier.
- Risk of implying guaranteed results.
- Risk of implying diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention.
- Risk of unfair comparison.

## Visual Risk Review

Watch for:

- Extreme before/after visuals.
- Unrealistic transformations.
- Fake screenshots.
- Fake testimonials.
- Fake media logos or awards.
- Unsafe use of product.
- Sensitive body imagery.
- Fear-based framing.

## TikTok Fit Review

Score:

- Hook clarity.
- First 3 seconds.
- Mobile readability.
- Native platform feel.
- Product visibility.
- Proof strength.
- CTA clarity.
- Rewatch value.
- Compliance risk.

## QC Score Guide

- 5: ready to test.
- 4: minor edits.
- 3: usable after revision.
- 2: concept risk or weak proof.
- 1: do not launch.

## QC Output Format

Return:

- Overall score.
- Launch recommendation.
- Top strengths.
- Required fixes.
- Optional improvements.
- Claims to verify.
- Lines or visuals to remove.
