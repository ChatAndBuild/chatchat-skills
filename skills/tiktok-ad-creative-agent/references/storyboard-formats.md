# Storyboard Formats

Use beat sheets that are easy for an editor, creator, or AI video agent to execute.

## Universal Beat Fields

Each beat should include:

- Time range.
- Purpose.
- Visual action.
- Camera direction.
- On-screen copy.
- Voiceover copy.
- Product exposure.
- Sound or editing cue.
- CTA or transition.
- Risk note when relevant.

## 15 Second Format

Best for simple offers, retargeting, or one proof point.

1. 0-2s hook.
2. 2-6s product reveal or problem setup.
3. 6-11s demo or proof.
4. 11-15s CTA and product lockup.

## 30 Second Format

Best default for paid social testing.

1. 0-3s hook.
2. 3-8s problem or desire.
3. 8-16s product mechanism or demo.
4. 16-24s proof, comparison, or routine.
5. 24-30s offer and CTA.

## 45 Second Format

Best for education, premium products, or multiple benefits.

1. 0-4s hook.
2. 4-10s audience problem.
3. 10-18s product reveal.
4. 18-28s mechanism and proof.
5. 28-38s outcome or lifestyle payoff.
6. 38-45s offer, CTA, product lockup.

## 60 Second Format

Best for complex products, high-ticket products, or founder-led creative.

1. 0-4s hook.
2. 4-12s context and problem.
3. 12-22s story or mechanism.
4. 22-36s demo and proof.
5. 36-48s objections and answer.
6. 48-60s offer, CTA, product lockup.

## Storyboard Rules

- Keep one primary idea per ad.
- Show the product early unless the suspense is intentional.
- Use on-screen copy to compress meaning, not to repeat every spoken line.
- Preserve TikTok safe areas for captions and UI.
- Put exact claims, legal lines, prices, and brand marks in editing notes, not AI visual prompts.
