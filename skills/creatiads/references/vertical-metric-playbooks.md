# Vertical Metric Playbooks

Use this reference after advertiser type classification and before formal reporting or optimization recommendations.

## Universal Core

Every analysis keeps these visible:

| Bucket | TikTok |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery | spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, reach, frequency |
| Result | result, conversion, cost per result, cost per conversion, conversion rate, value when active |
| Structure | advertiser, campaign, ad group, ad, objective, optimization goal, billing event, campaign automation type |
| Breakdown | country/region, age, gender, placement, platform/device when supported |
| Creative | 2s/6s views, quartiles, average play, engaged view, preview evidence |

The five core business report fields are: impressions, clicks, spend, conversion/result, and revenue/value. If revenue/value is missing, run a relevant metric probe for the account or mark it as unavailable after probe evidence.

## Ecommerce

Goal: purchase value, catalog/product quality, ROAS, landing-page funnel.

Prioritize:

- TikTok: onsite purchases ROAS, onsite shopping ROAS, shop gross revenue, onsite purchase, onsite add to cart, checkout initiation, product detail view, complete payment, total purchase value.

Analysis focus:

- ROAS/value vs CPA.
- View-content to cart to checkout to purchase leakage where active.
- SKU or landing URL concentration.
- Creative clarity and click-to-purchase quality.

Avoid claiming margin, profit, new-customer quality, or true incrementality unless the user provided first-party data.

## Utility Or W2A

Goal: store/deeplink path quality, install, activation, trial, subscription.

Prioritize:

- TikTok: onsite destination visits, onsite download start, real-time app install, SKAN app install, app install, registration, login, trial, subscribe, onsite form, subscribe value.

Analysis focus:

- Web-to-app redirect evidence and store destination fit.
- Click to destination to install to registration/trial/subscribe.
- SKAN/SAN availability and delay risk.
- Separate web traffic diagnostics from app conversion diagnostics.

Treat Adjust, Appsflyer, OneLink, Branch, or self-owned domains that end at App Store or Google Play as W2A/app paths.

## Short Drama

Goal: attention quality, episode hook, install/register/subscribe/purchase proxy.

Prioritize TikTok:

- paid engaged view 15s, engaged view 15s.
- 2s/6s views and p25/p50/p75/p100.
- subscribe value day 0, day 1, day 6, total subscribe value.
- install, registration, subscribe, purchase, purchase value when active.

Analysis focus:

- Opening hook retention.
- Cheap-click low-retention traps.
- Top conversion creatives vs top retention creatives.
- Episode promise and subscription value chain.

## Casual Game

Goal: install volume plus tutorial, retention, and monetization proxies.

Prioritize TikTok:

- real-time app install, SKAN app install, app install.
- complete tutorial, achieve level, day 7 retention.
- in-app ad impression/click, purchase value, custom app event value.

Analysis focus:

- CPI vs retained/monetized user proxy.
- Gameplay clarity in creative.
- Geo/device/placement quality.
- Avoid scaling cheap installs with weak tutorial or retention evidence.

## Midcore Or Hardcore Game

Goal: quality install, role creation, level, achievement, retention, purchase.

Prioritize TikTok:

- real-time app install, SKAN app install.
- create gamerole, achieve level, unlock achievement, day 7 retention.
- purchase and purchase value.

Analysis focus:

- Downstream quality over CPI.
- Creative promise vs genre audience fit.
- Audience segments with role/level quality.

## Financial Leads

Goal: qualified application, credit, disbursement, and compliant lead quality.

Prioritize:

- TikTok: form, onsite form, button click, direct message conversation, loan apply, loan credit, loan disbursement, sales lead, registration.

Analysis focus:

- CPL vs apply/credit/disbursement proxy.
- Cheap unqualified lead traps.
- Geo/device/placement compliance-safe traffic quality.

## Novel

Goal: story hook, install/register/start trial/subscribe/purchase proxy.

Prioritize TikTok:

- paid engaged view 15s, engaged view 15s.
- subscribe value day 0, day 1, day 6, total subscribe value.
- install, registration, trial, subscribe, purchase, day 7 retention.

Analysis focus:

- Hook and premise resonance.
- Install to subscription quality.
- Creative refresh ranking by retention and subscription proxy.

## Entertainment

Goal: attention, engagement, registration/login/subscribe/purchase, live or messaging paths.

Prioritize TikTok:

- live views, live effective views.
- direct message conversations.
- follows, profile visits, engaged view 15s.
- registration, login, subscribe, purchase.

Analysis focus:

- Separate cheap engagement from meaningful activation.
- Placement and creative resonance.
- Live or messaging handoff quality.

## Search Arbitrage

Goal: click quality, landing engagement, margin discipline when external margin is provided.

Prioritize TikTok:

- button click, clicks, search, CTR, CPC, CPM.
- placement type, device, country.
- onsite destination visits and conversion/value proxy when active.

Analysis focus:

- Cheap clicks vs qualified downstream action.
- Device/placement/geography cost mix.
- Do not claim margin without external revenue or margin data.

## Social

Goal: install/register/login/subscribe and meaningful engagement.

Prioritize TikTok:

- live views, live effective views, direct message conversations.
- follows, profile visits, engaged view 15s.
- likes, comments, shares.
- registration, login, subscribe, purchase.

Analysis focus:

- Activation quality, not installs alone.
- Social proof and audience fit.
- Engagement that assists registration or subscription.

## Agency Or Mixed

Goal: classify each account, then compare within comparable groups.

Rules:

- Do not rank mixed vertical accounts by one CPA or ROAS.
- Use core metrics for cross-account health.
- Use vertical metrics only inside each account's resolved type.
- Show coverage state per account.
- For TikTok, include campaign automation type, placement type, billing event, cash spend, and voucher spend when available.
