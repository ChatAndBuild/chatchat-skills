## Platform-Level Benchmark Context (2025 Update)

> Use these as directional context when interpreting performance, not as hard targets.

| Metric | Reported Range (2025) | Best Estimate | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPM | $4.80–$13.26 | $8–$10 for US performance brands | Lebesgue ($13.26) skews US/DTC; global/mixed samples show $4.80–$9 |
| CPC | $0.20–$2.00 | Median ~$0.50 (Varos, US panel) | Varos panel is DTC/e-comm heavy; brand/awareness CPC typically higher |
| CTR | 0.5–1.5% | ~0.84% for conversion-optimized campaigns | Awareness campaigns have lower CTR by design |
| Conversion Rate | 0.3–5.0% | ~0.5–2.0% for most verticals | Enormous variance by vertical, price point, and funnel stage |
| CPA | $13–$100+ | ~$32 US average per Lebesgue | Extremely variable — meaningless as a cross-vertical average |

> **Methodology note:** Every third-party benchmark provider (Lebesgue, Varos, Triple Whale,
> AdBacklog) uses a self-selected panel skewing toward DTC, e-commerce, and performance brands.
> These underrepresent large brand advertisers, CPG, finance, and automotive — which have
> structurally different CPMs and CTRs. No publicly available TikTok benchmark dataset uses
> a randomly sampled, representative panel. All benchmarks are directional reference points,
> not authoritative ground truth. When vertical benchmarks are available, prefer those over
> cross-vertical averages.
>
